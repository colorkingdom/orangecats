How to read this:
O is the red allele and o is the non-red allele.
'Non-red' represents black or black tabby; other color genes may change those colors to blue or blue tabby, seal point or seal tabby point, etc.
The male frequencies are p and 1−p; the female frequencies are p², 2p(1−p), and (1−p)².
These are expected proportions from the allele frequency, not a prediction for a particular litter.
